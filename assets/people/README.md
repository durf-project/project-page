# Photos of people

Square JPEGs, 400 x 400 pixels, named after the person in lower case with hyphens
(`hilde-van-wijngaarden.jpg`). They are used by the people dialogs in "The federation and its
circles" (`content/people.json`, field `photo`) and by the Federation Day slides. When a file is
missing, the page tries `photoSource` and otherwise shows initials.

| File | Person | Source |

| ingeborg-verheul.jpg | Ingeborg Verheul | amsterdamtimemachine.nl |
