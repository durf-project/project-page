DURF — Dutch Repository Federation

Making Dutch research global, open and resilient.

About the project

DURF coordinates the Netherlands' existing Open Science infrastructure — more than 70 institutional repositories and CRIS systems, the OpenAIRE Graph, the Netherlands Research Portal, the KB e-Depot and the NBN resolver — under one shared governance layer. DURF does not build a new central system: it connects what already exists, through shared agreements between institutions, across six themes (see the table below).

The project runs from 1 June 2026 to 31 May 2030, funded by NWO under the Open Science Infrastructure programme. It brings together seven grant-recipient institutions and fourteen cooperation partners, with a mandate unanimously endorsed by the Dutch university rectors.
