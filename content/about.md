# About DURF

The **Dutch Repository Federation (DURF)** is a four-year programme (June 2026 to May 2030) in which Dutch university libraries, the KB National Library, SURF and OpenAIRE work together on a shared, mature and resilient infrastructure for Dutch scientific output.

DURF reinforces the [Netherlands Research Portal](https://netherlands.openaire.eu/) and the institutional repository and CRIS landscape, so that Dutch research output is more robust, sovereign and findable. Six themes run in parallel over the four years.

Together, the libraries are the custodians of the *Dutch Scholarly Communication Corpus*. The [position paper](https://durf-project.github.io/position-paper/) explains why that matters and what the signatories commit to.
