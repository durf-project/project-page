## The people behind DURF

DURF is run by people from the university libraries, the KB National Library, SURF and OpenAIRE. The project lead and the theme leads coordinate the work, supporting roles keep it running, and a steering group oversees it on behalf of the partners.

### Cooperation partners

These institutions support DURF in kind (section 4.2 of the [project proposal](https://communities.surf.nl/files/Artikel/download/OS%20infra%20full-proposal%20DURF%20Dutch%20Repository%20Federation_ContactandFinancialinforemoved.pdf)) and, with the co-applicants, are the custodians of the Dutch Scholarly Communication Corpus.

- Leiden University
- University of Twente
- Utrecht University
- University of Humanistic Studies
- Wageningen University & Research
- Amsterdam UMC, for the university medical centres (NFU)
- Saxion University of Applied Sciences, for the universities of applied sciences

The co-applicants are the KB National Library, SURF, TU Delft, the University of Amsterdam, Leiden University, VU Amsterdam and Wageningen University & Research. OpenAIRE takes part as a third party.
