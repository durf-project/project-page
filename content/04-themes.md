## One federation, six themes

The six themes run in parallel from June 2026 to May 2030. Each has its own colour, which you will find back in the [roadmap](https://durf-project.github.io/durf-gantt/) and the [dashboards](https://durf-project.github.io/dashboards/). Goals and partners follow the project roadmap; the people behind each theme are in [our team](#team).

<!-- One "###" card per theme. Keep "theme: N" straight under the heading: it sets the colour. -->

### Governance of the Federation and Netherlands Research Portal

theme: 1

A sustainable governance framework for the Dutch federated research information ecosystem, including the parties that maintain repository and CRIS systems, the e-Depot and the OpenAIRE Graph.

**Leads:** Simone Kortekaas (KB National Library) and Maurice Vanderfeesten (SURF).<br>
**Partners:** SURF (community management, legal advice, project assistance).

### Metadata Quality Improvement

theme: 2

Repository and CRIS systems adopt the updated metadata and exchange standards for high-quality research metadata that comply with international guidelines and meet national requirements.

**Lead:** Rutger de Jong (Leiden University).<br>
**Partners:** WUR (tech lead), SURF (community management, legal advice, project assistance, architect), KB (WISH).

### Full Text Capture

theme: 3

Comprehensive full-text availability in Dutch repository and CRIS systems, primarily for preservation, and secondarily for text mining, AI usage and Taverne mechanisms.

**Lead:** Pascal Braak (University of Amsterdam).<br>
**Partners:** WUR (tech lead), SURF (community management, legal advice, project assistance).

### KB National Library e-Depot Archiving

theme: 4

Comprehensive preservation of Dutch scholarly output in the KB e-Depot.

**Lead:** Ingeborg Verheul (KB National Library).<br>
**Partners:** WUR (tech lead), SURF (legal).

### Metadata Distribution & Discovery Optimization

theme: 5

Increase the visibility of Dutch research content in major international indexes.

**Lead:** Rutger de Jong (Leiden University).<br>
**Partners:** WUR (tech lead).

### NL Research Portal Update

theme: 6

A fully functional, user-centred Netherlands Research Portal that serves as the primary discovery point for Dutch scholarly output.

**Lead:** vacant.<br>
**Partners:** none listed yet.
