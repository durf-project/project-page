# The six themes

<!-- Goals and leads follow the project roadmap (durf-project/durf-gantt). Keep the "N." numbers
     at the start of each heading: the page uses them for the theme colours. -->

## 1. Governance of the Federation and Netherlands Research Portal

A sustainable governance framework established for the Dutch federated research information ecosystem, including parties maintaining Repository/CRIS systems, e-Depot and OpenAIRE Graph. **Lead:** TU Delft.

## 2. Metadata Quality Improvement

Repository/CRIS systems have adopted the updated metadata and exchange standards for high-quality research metadata that comply with international guidelines and meet national requirements. **Lead:** Leiden.

## 3. Full Text Capture

Comprehensive full-text availability in Dutch repository/CRIS systems, primarily for preservation, and secondarily for text mining, AI usage and Taverne mechanisms. **Lead:** UvA.

## 4. KB National Library e-Depot Archiving

Comprehensive preservation of Dutch scientific output in the KB e-Depot. **Lead:** KB National Library.

## 5. Metadata Distribution & Discovery Optimization

Increase the visibility of Dutch research content in major international indexes. **Lead:** Leiden.

## 6. NL Research Portal Update

Deliver a fully functional, user-centred Netherlands Research Portal that serves as the primary discovery point for Dutch research outputs. **Lead:** TU Delft.

Progress per theme is tracked in the [DURF dashboards](https://durf-project.github.io/dashboards/) and planned in the [roadmap](https://durf-project.github.io/durf-gantt/).
