# Documents and tools

- [Position paper](https://durf-project.github.io/position-paper/): why Dutch libraries should commit to a shared infrastructure, and what they pledge per theme.
- [Roadmap](https://durf-project.github.io/durf-gantt/): the six themes, their activities and months as a Gantt chart.
- [Data contracts](https://durf-project.github.io/durf-data-mesh-architecture/): data governance for the federation, with example contracts.
- [Dashboards](https://durf-project.github.io/dashboards/): progress monitoring per theme, with embeddable charts.
- [Netherlands Research Portal](https://netherlands.openaire.eu/): the national discovery point for Dutch research output.
- [DURF on GitHub](https://github.com/durf-project): all sources; raise an issue in the relevant repository to give feedback.
